Privacy & Confidentiality Notice

Last updated: 26 October 2026

This page applies to Bolgsvenia Global, a UK-based independent service business. Please contact us if you need this information in an accessible format.

Privacy & Confidentiality Notice Terms & Conditions Legal information

1. Who we are and how to contact us

Bolgsvenia Global is the trading name of Carlos Irias Romero, a sole trader established in the United Kingdom and based in Oxfordshire. We provide translation, website development and appointment-based remote IT support. For direct enquiries we normally act as the data controller; where we process documents strictly on a business client’s documented instructions, we may act as a processor. Contact: bolgsvenia.global@bolgsvenia.com. ICO registration reference published by the business: ZC230699. Our general business location is not a postal correspondence address; contact us to arrange a suitable address for formal correspondence.

2. Information we may collect

For enquiries: your name, email address and, if you choose a call or text response, telephone number; service required, language direction, type of document, intended use, approximate length, timescale, project requirements and messages. For accepted work: supplied documents, correspondence, translations or project files, delivery details and records needed for invoicing and accounting. Website and form providers may also process IP addresses, device/browser details, submission times and security logs. Information generally comes from you or an authorised person, agency or client acting for you.

3. Why we use the information and our lawful bases

We use enquiries to assess requests and provide personalised quotations (steps before entering a contract); accepted work to deliver services, communicate and deal with corrections (contract); accounts, tax and other mandatory records (legal obligation); security, fraud prevention, quality and complaints management (legitimate interests, weighed against individual rights); and optional AI chat only after affirmative opt-in (consent). We do not use enquiry details for unrelated advertising.

4. Sensitive material and third-party information

Translations can involve identification, health, financial, legal or other sensitive information. Do not send confidential documents, passwords, one-time codes or third-party personal data through AI chat or an initial general enquiry. Contact us to arrange an appropriate route for sensitive documents. When legally required, we identify the separate condition necessary for special-category or criminal-offence information; a business client instructing us as processor is responsible for its own lawful basis.

5. Website forms and contact choices

Forms use Web3Forms to handle and forward the submission. Your selected contact method (email, call or SMS) is used to respond to your enquiry, not for marketing. We may receive technical anti-spam and delivery metadata. Submitting a request does not create a contract or automatically accept work. For the initial enquiry, only supply information needed for an assessment. You can contact us by email instead of using a form.

6. Optional AI assistant

The optional Harbour assistant is distinct from the enquiry forms. Only if you opt in does a message and recent conversation context pass from our website through Netlify to OpenAI to generate a reply. You can decline or withdraw this consent using the chat’s privacy settings; the ordinary forms remain available. The visible conversation is kept in that browser tab’s memory, not a Bolgsvenia Global chat database, but infrastructure/providers may keep security or abuse-prevention records. We request store: false from the OpenAI Responses API; this must not be understood as a promise of zero provider retention. Do not put private documents or personal identifiers in chat. The assistant cannot submit enquiries, bind us to a contract or determine prices. AI-specific privacy information.

7. Who may receive information

As necessary: Web3Forms for enquiry delivery; GitHub Pages (GitHub) for static website hosting; Netlify for the optional AI serverless function; email/business-productivity providers including Google Workspace; OpenAI only if you activate AI chat; accounting, banking, payment and storage providers; and authorised professional collaborators or reviewers where agreed and governed by suitable confidentiality arrangements. We may disclose data if legally required. We do not sell personal information.

8. Transfers outside the United Kingdom

Hosting, email, forms, AI and other service suppliers may process information or enable support access outside the UK. Where UK data-protection rules require safeguards for a restricted international transfer, we use an applicable UK adequacy regulation, appropriate contractual safeguards or another lawful transfer mechanism. Email us for information about relevant provider arrangements.

9. Retention and deletion

Enquiries not leading to work and quotation-only files are normally retained for no longer than 90 days after the last meaningful contact, unless needed for a legitimate unresolved matter. Files and working material for completed assignments are normally held for up to 12 months for corrections, reissues or support, unless law or agreement requires otherwise. Invoices, tax and payment records are retained for statutory periods; disputes for as long as reasonably necessary. Provider logs and backups may follow their own documented cycles; we cannot promise immediate deletion from every provider backup.

10. Security

We use proportionate security such as restricted access, account authentication, device maintenance and limiting the information collected. No internet or email service is completely risk-free. Never share account passwords, security codes or remote-access credentials through the chatbot or public forms.

11. Your rights — including your right to object

Depending on the context and lawful basis, you may request access, correction, erasure, restriction, portability, and object to processing. You have the right to object to processing based on legitimate interests. You may withdraw consent to optional AI processing at any time without affecting processing lawfully performed beforehand. We may need to verify your identity and may rely on lawful exceptions. Contact us by email; requests are normally answered within one month, subject to legal extensions.

12. Complaints

You can raise privacy concerns directly with us. You may also complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO). Where applicable, an EU/EEA supervisory authority may also be relevant.

13. Cookies and technical data

The site is designed without advertising, analytics-tracking or marketing cookies. Hosting, abuse-prevention and form providers may use technical logs or strictly necessary security measures. If non-essential tracking is introduced, we will provide the relevant information and obtain consent where required.

14. Marketing and automated decisions

We do not use enquiry details for a marketing mailing list without an appropriate lawful basis. AI chat gives general information only; it does not make solely automated decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects.

15. Is providing information compulsory?

Fields needed to respond to an enquiry are identified in the form. The initial enquiry is voluntary, but without appropriate contact details or instructions we may not be able to reply or prepare a quotation. Further information may be needed only after you and the business agree to proceed.

16. Keeping this notice accurate

This notice reflects the website services and providers identified above. We will amend it if we materially change how personal information is used, and we will explain significant changes before starting a new use where required.

17. Contact for privacy matters

Please email bolgsvenia.global@bolgsvenia.com about your personal information, international-transfer safeguards or other privacy questions.
