GOOD TO KNOW

Questions before ordering?

Do I need a certified translation?

That depends on the organisation receiving the document. Please check its requirements; a UK translation certification statement can be discussed where appropriate.

Can you provide a sworn translation for use in Spain?

Some procedures in Spain require a sworn translator authorised in Spain. Bolgsvenia Global does not itself claim that authorisation; we can discuss whether support through an authorised professional is available.

Must I upload a passport or certificate with my enquiry?

No. You can describe the document or optionally attach one file up to 5 MB. Avoid uploading sensitive identity papers unless needed; contact us first if in doubt.

Will the translation be accepted by every authority?

Each authority sets its own requirements. We can tailor the service to the stated requirements, but we cannot guarantee acceptance by every receiving body.
