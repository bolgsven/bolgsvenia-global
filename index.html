Pricing

Prices depend on the language direction, document type, length, formatting, urgency and whether a translation certification statement is required.

Standard Translation

£0.08 per source word, subject to a minimum charge of £35. The final quote is confirmed after reviewing the document and before work begins.

Translation Certification Statement for UK Use

From £40 where the receiving organisation or authority requests a written certification statement for a translation intended for use in the UK and accepts this form of certification.

Complex or Specially Formatted Documents

Scanned documents, complex formatting, tables, handwritten text or special requirements are quoted individually after the material has been reviewed.

About Bolgsvenia Global

Bolgsvenia Global is a UK-based translation service providing Spanish ↔ English translations for individuals, businesses and professional clients.

The service focuses particularly on personal, administrative, business and official documentation, with attention to accuracy, clear presentation, terminological consistency and confidential handling.

Each assignment is reviewed individually to confirm the requirements, delivery time and any certification needs before work begins.

Translation certification and Spanish sworn translations

For translations intended for use in the UK, Bolgsvenia Global can provide a translation certification statement where appropriate and where accepted by the receiving organisation or authority.

Where a document specifically requires a sworn translation for use in Spain, the final certification must be completed by a sworn translator or sworn translator-interpreter authorised in Spain. Bolgsvenia Global is not authorised in Spain under either sworn title.

Bolgsvenia Global provides translation services and does not provide legal advice.
