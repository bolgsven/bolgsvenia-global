COMMON QUESTIONS

No. This is appointment-based remote assistance for defined tasks, not a continuously staffed outsourced service desk.

Will you need my password?

Do not send passwords or authentication codes through the form. Any access or authentication arrangements are agreed separately and should follow secure practices.

Can you help in Spanish?

Yes. You can describe your enquiry in English or Spanish.
